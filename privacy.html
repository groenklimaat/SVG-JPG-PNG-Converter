Privacy Policy

PNG Converter — SVG, JPG & PNG to PNG

1. Summary

This application processes all your images entirely inside your own browser. No files, images, or personal data are ever sent to or stored on any server.

2. What data do we process?

  • Images you upload: processed only locally in your browser's memory (via the HTML5 Canvas element). They never leave your device.
  • Preferences (size, color, transparency, language): stored only during your session or in your browser's localStorage. Not sent anywhere.
  • No account, no tracking, no cookies for ads or analytics.

3. External services

To create ZIP files, the JavaScript library JSZip is loaded from a CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). Technically, the CDN sees your IP address, as with any website loading external resources. No images are ever sent to this CDN.

If you host this app via GitHub Pages, GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP address, timestamp) when you visit the page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

4. Offline usage (PWA)

The app uses a Service Worker to cache components locally so the app also works offline. This cache is stored on your own device and can be cleared via your browser settings.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

Because we do not collect or store any personal data, there is nothing to access, modify, or delete on our side. You retain full control over your own files.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. The most recent version is always available on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.
